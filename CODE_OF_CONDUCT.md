# Code of Conduct

CodexKit is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free
community for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent while addressing impact.
- Focus feedback on ideas, code, and evidence.
- Protect private information and credentials.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, sexualized
conduct, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the repository maintainer through the GitHub
profile at <https://github.com/manhvann>. Reports will be reviewed fairly and
confidentially where practical. Maintainers may edit or remove content and may
restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
